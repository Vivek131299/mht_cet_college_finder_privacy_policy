Privacy Policy
MHT CET College Finder
Last Updated: February 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MHT CET College Finder ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Android application. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

The App is developed and maintained by Vivek Bhavsar ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how the App works.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The only data you voluntarily enter includes:

  • Your MHT CET percentile score (used locally on your device to filter college results)
  • Preference settings such as selected branches, categories, and buffer values
  • Colleges you choose to save to your personal saved list

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, the following information may be collected automatically:

  • Device information such as device model and Android version (used for crash reporting and compatibility)
  • App usage data such as which screens are visited and how features are used (collected via Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reports and error logs to help us improve app stability (collected via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Push notification tokens for sending app notifications (collected via Firebase Cloud Messaging)

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your camera, microphone, or media files
  • Any payment or financial information
  • Any information from other apps on your device

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide and improve the core functionality of the App
  • To display relevant college cutoff data based on your entered percentile
  • To remember your saved colleges and preferences between sessions
  • To send you important notifications such as app updates or new data availability
  • To analyze app usage patterns and fix bugs to improve user experience
  • To display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the free availability of the App

4. Data Storage and Retention

4.1 Local Storage

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

  • Your saved colleges list
  • Your recent search history
  • Your filter preferences and view settings
  • Downloaded CAP round cutoff data

This data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear the App's data from your device settings.

4.2 Remote Data

College cutoff data is downloaded from a private GitHub repository maintained by us. This data contains only publicly available MHT CET cutoff information and no personal data. The App uses a read-only access token to fetch this data securely.

4.3 Third-Party Storage

Data collected by third-party services (Firebase, AdMob) is stored on their respective servers subject to their own privacy policies. We do not have direct control over this data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data:

5.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device's Google settings (Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization).

AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5.2 Firebase (Google)

We use the following Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics: Collects anonymized usage statistics to help us understand how the App is used
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Enables push notifications. Your device token is used solely to deliver notifications

Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5.3 GitHub

The App connects to GitHub's API to download college cutoff data. GitHub may log API requests including IP addresses. We use a read-only access token with minimal permissions limited to accessing only our data repository.

GitHub Privacy Policy: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement

6. App Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to download college cutoff data from GitHub and to display advertisements
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required to send you push notifications about app updates and new data (Android 13 and above). You can deny this permission and the App will still function normally

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for students preparing for MHT CET admissions and is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App does not require any personal information to function.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at the email address below and we will promptly delete such information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information associated with the App:

  • All data downloads from GitHub use HTTPS encrypted connections
  • The GitHub access token used in the App has read-only permissions limited to a single repository
  • No sensitive personal data is transmitted or stored by us
  • Local data stored on your device is protected by Android's built-in app sandboxing

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of data processed by third-party services such as Firebase and AdMob.

9. Your Rights and Choices

9.1 Data Deletion

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no account to delete. All locally stored data (saved colleges, preferences, search history) can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data from your device settings (Settings > Apps > MHT CET College Finder > Clear Data).

9.2 Notifications

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings (Settings > Apps > MHT CET College Finder > Notifications).

9.3 Advertising

You can opt out of personalized advertisements through your device's Google settings. The App will continue to show non-personalized ads.

10. Data Accuracy Disclaimer

The college cutoff data displayed in this App is sourced from publicly available MHT CET CAP round results. While we strive to keep this data accurate and up to date, we do not guarantee the accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of the information.

This App is for reference purposes only. Always verify admission cutoffs and eligibility from official sources such as the State Common Entrance Test Cell (CET Cell), Maharashtra before making any admission decisions.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through a push notification or an in-app message. The updated policy will always be accessible at the link provided on our Play Store listing.

Your continued use of the App after any changes to this Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
